Questions?

Frequently asked

How long does setup take?

We arrive about 45–60 minutes before your booth time to set up, so everything is ready when guests arrive. Your package hours start when the booth opens.

How much space do you need?

Roughly 2.5 × 2.5 metres of flat floor space, ideally near a standard power socket. It works indoors or outdoors in the shade. Let us know your space and we'll confirm.

Are the prints really unlimited?

Yes! Guests can take as many photos and prints as they like during your booked hours. You'll also get a link to all the digital photos after the party.

Can the print match our party theme?

Absolutely. With the Party and Milestone packages we design a print with the birthday star's name, age and party theme, whether it's dinosaurs, princesses, superheroes or gold and black.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover all of Dubai. For parties outside Dubai, a small travel fee may apply. Just message us your location.

How do I book and secure my date?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your date, area and package. We'll confirm availability and the details, and a small deposit secures your date. Weekends in party season (October–April) fill up fast!
